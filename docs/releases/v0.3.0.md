# VibeCoding 0.3.0

Released 02.11.2026

## What's new

- The page of a chain shows where it is: its project and thread, «Неразобранное», «Вне проектов» or
  «Вне проектов · убрана вручную».
- «Перенести»: move a chain by hand into a thread of any of your projects, into its «Неразобранное» or
  into a new thread you name. The chain then shows «Вручную».
- A chain placed by hand stays where you put it: a new email in it, a mailbox reading and «Заполнить»
  never move it.
- «Убрать из проекта» takes a chain out of its project for good: the app never places it again by
  itself; you can still move it back by hand.
- «Заполнить» on a filled project keeps the chains placed by hand and their threads, and lays out the
  rest again.
- Every chain in a project tree and in «Неразобранное» shows whether it was placed by hand («Вручную»)
  or by the engine («Движок text-1 · сходство N%»).

## Under the hood

- Database migration 0006 lets a placement be made by hand or mark a chain taken out; it runs when the

- When two chains join, a placement made by hand wins over the engine's.
