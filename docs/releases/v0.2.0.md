# VibeCoding 0.2.0

Released 02.11.2026

## What's new

- Mailboxes: connect several mailboxes over IMAP with an app password. The app only reads them: it
  never sends, deletes or marks an email, and unread emails stay unread. The password is stored
  encrypted and deleted when the mailbox is disconnected; the emails read so far stay.
- Inbox and Sent of the last 12 months are read in the background every 5 minutes, and at once on
  «Обновить».
- «Переписка»: the emails of all mailboxes are joined into reply chains; an email present in two
  mailboxes is shown once.
- «Проекты»: create a project with a name and a description. «Заполнить» finds the project's
  correspondence and lays it out as a tree: counterparties, their threads and the chains in them.
  Chains the app is not sure of go to «Неразобранное».
- Every placed chain says why it is there: «Движок text-1 · сходство N%».
- New emails take their place in the tree after each reading; a chain already placed never moves.
- «Заполнить» on a filled project asks «Пересобрать проект?» and gives the same tree from the same
  emails.
- The placement runs inside the app: no email text is sent anywhere.

## Under the hood

- A local text-similarity engine `text-1` (TF-IDF over Russian and English word stems); its version is
  stored with every placement, so a future engine change is visible in the result.
- Database migrations 0004 and 0005 add mailboxes, emails, reply chains, projects, threads and
  placements; they run when the backend starts.
- A local demo mail server (GreenMail) with prepared Russian correspondence: `just demo-mail`.

## How to update

- Add `MAILBOX_ENCRYPTION_KEY` to `.env`: a Fernet key, generated as `.env.example` shows. Keep it:
  losing or changing it makes every connected mailbox ask for a new app password.
- For the demo mail, add `DEMO_MAIL_PASSWORD` from `.env.example`.
