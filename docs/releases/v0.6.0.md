# VibeCoding 0.6.0

Released 04.11.2026

## What's new

- The connect form on «Почтовые ящики» starts with «Адрес почты». An address on yandex.ru, mail.ru,
  gmail.com or icloud.com picks its service and fills the server and the port; you can pick another
  service by hand. Any other domain picks «Другой» with a hint where to find the IMAP server.
- The server, the port and a login other than the address sit under «Настройки сервера».
- Each of these services shows «Как подключить» with short steps (turn on IMAP, create an app
  password) and a link to its official help, opened in a new tab.
- Above the form the page says the app only reads the mailbox and why an app password is needed.
- If a mailbox cannot sign in, its row shows «Как исправить» with the steps of its service; a failed
  connection keeps the steps on view.
- The service is picked inside the app: the address is not sent anywhere before «Подключить».
