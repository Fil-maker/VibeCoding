# VibeCoding 0.5.0

Released 04.11.2026

## What's new

- After you create or edit a rule, «Правила» lists the chains it would move, from where to where;
  «Применить» moves them at once, «Не сейчас» moves nothing.
- A rule applied this way moves only the chains it wins and only those outside projects or placed by
  the engine or a rule; chains placed by hand or taken out of projects stay. A thread left empty
  disappears.
- Every page after sign-in shares one header with «Проекты», «Переписка», «Почтовые ящики»,
  «Правила», your name and «Выйти»; on a narrow screen it folds into «Меню».
- A guest sees a landing page at the start address; after sign-in, «Первые шаги» guide you until a
  mailbox is connected and a project is created and filled.
- Loading, empty and failed pages look the same everywhere, and each browser tab is titled
  «<page> — VibeCoding».
- A dark theme follows your system setting; buttons, placement badges and colors are consistent and
  readable in both themes.
- Counterparties and «Неразобранное» fold and unfold; tables turn into cards on a narrow screen.

## Fixes

- Signing out lands on the landing page.
- The empty «Проекты» shows «Создать проект» once.

## Breaking changes

None.

## Under the hood

- No database migration in this version.
